Connect your car

Use your car maker’s own API key to connect your car to your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok…). Beta.

Škoda: get your key (2 minutes, in the phone app)
  1. Open the MyŠkoda app → Profil → Chytrá domácnost (Smart home).
  2. Tap Vytvořit klíč (Create key), name it e.g. “MissingMCP”, then Kopírovat klíč (Copy key).
  3. Copy the VIN from the same screen.
Keys can only be created in the app, last about 6 months, and can be deleted there any time.
What happens to your data
  • Your API key and VIN are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on this server, so it can reach your car on your behalf. They are never shared with your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok…) or its maker.
  • Car data is passed through on demand and is not stored by this gateway.
  • Škoda’s API is for personal use; this is an unofficial, free beta. Your email is used only for important news about this connector.
