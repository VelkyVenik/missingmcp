Subject: MissingMCP: Garmin sign-ins should finally just work

Hi,

TL;DR: I believe I've finally fixed the Garmin sign-in problems caused by Garmin's rate limiting.

I'm genuinely thrilled that in just three months, almost 2,000 of you use missingmcp every week. Let's see how many it takes for Garmin to ship a native MCP of their own :) Until then, missingmcp is here for you.

Here's what's new.

⌚ GARMIN
The biggest challenge has been load. This service started as something for me and a few friends, so we've been scaling it step by step as usage grows. Compute and memory are the easy part; Garmin's limits are the hard one. Garmin's sign-in was rate-limiting the shared cloud address the service runs on, so as of today, Garmin sign-ins go out through our own dedicated addresses, spread between them, with automatic fallback if one gets limited. I'm curious how far this takes us. I'm watching the logs closely and would love your feedback.

One tip: Garmin also limits repeated sign-ins of the same account. If you see "Garmin is temporarily rate-limiting new sign-ins" right after connecting, just wait a minute and try again.

💪 WHOOP
WHOOP's developer support has been incredibly responsive, and after two months they approved raising our user limit from 10 to 100. So if you've been waiting, there are plenty of free spots now.
